01 / ABOUT

Curiosity, grounded
in real systems.

I am a Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering graduate from the University of Dhaka and a Research Assistant on CogniCare, a UGC-funded adaptive robotics project.

I led the development of CogniCare’s software and adaptive decision-making components, from belief-state estimation and online planning to experimental logging and integration with the physical robot.

My broader interests include robot learning, computer vision, and sequential decision-making under uncertainty. I am interested in how robotic systems can make useful decisions when the world—and the people they interact with—cannot be observed completely.

CogniCare

Personalized cognitive rehabilitation under uncertainty.

An assistive robotic system that adapts cognitive-task selection and difficulty using a POMDP-based personalization framework and online POMCP planning.

My contribution Primary developer of the software and adaptive decision-making components.

Explore the work +
  • Implemented belief-state estimation, performance processing, and adaptive policy selection.
  • Developed experimental logging and validation pipelines.
  • Integrated rehabilitation software, interactive displays, sensing, and physical robot components through a communication and synchronization layer.
  • Evaluated the framework through simulation, controlled role-play experiments, and pilot-user studies.

Supervised by Dr. Sejuti Rahman and Md. Jubair Ahmed Sourov at the University of Dhaka.
