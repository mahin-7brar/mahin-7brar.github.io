Selected work

Assistive robotics

CogniCare: A Personalized Rehabilitation Robot for Mild Cognitive Impairment

Primary developer · Software and adaptive decision-making

Developed a personalization framework that adapts cognitive-task selection and difficulty using a POMDP and online POMCP planning.

  • Implemented belief estimation, performance processing, and adaptive policy selection.
  • Integrated rehabilitation software with displays, sensing, and the physical robot through a communication and synchronization layer.
  • Built experimental logging and validation workflows; evaluated through simulation, controlled role-play, and pilot-user studies.
In preparation

Manuscript planned for submission to AAMAS 2027.
