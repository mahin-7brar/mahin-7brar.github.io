UNIVERSITY OF DHAKA · UGC-FUNDED PROJECT
ADAPTIVE ROBOTICSApr 2025 – Present

CogniCare

A personalized rehabilitation robot for mild cognitive impairment.

An assistive robot that combines interactive cognitive tasks, sensing, and adaptive software to personalize rehabilitation. My work spans the software, the personalization framework, and integration with the physical system.

My contribution Primary developer of the software and adaptive decision-making components.

Explore the work +
  • Developed POMDP-based personalization with online POMCP planning to adapt task selection and difficulty under uncertainty in the user’s cognitive state.
  • Implemented belief-state estimation, performance processing, and adaptive policy selection.
  • Developed experimental logging and validation pipelines.
  • Integrated rehabilitation software, interactive displays, sensing, and physical robot components through a communication and synchronization layer.
  • Evaluated the framework through simulation, controlled role-play experiments, and pilot-user studies.

Supervised by Dr. Sejuti Rahman and Md. Jubair Ahmed Sourov at the University of Dhaka.
